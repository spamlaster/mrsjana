- **Sound transcriptions:** none checked yet (appendix A).

## 1. What the center is

The center practices **minimal pairs**: two real words that differ by one sound, such as *key* and *tea*. A child who says "tea" for "key" hears and practices the difference between the two words, one pair at a time, with lots of repetition.

A session goes like this:

1. Choose the sound pattern (for example Fronting).
2. Choose the sound and where it is in one tap: each sound card (for example /k/) has its own **Beginning of the word** and **End of the word** buttons.
3. Choose a word pair (for example *key / tea*).
4. Choose a game.
5. Play. Games are 20 turns (10 of each word) by default; tick "Fewer turns: 5 of each word" above the game before starting for 10 (Bubble Pop says "Fewer bubbles"). Each word comes up exactly half the time, in a shuffled order, and never more than three times in a row. In scored games, after each try the grown-up taps **Correct**, **Incorrect**, or **Cued**, and **Undo** takes back the last tap. The game rewards every turn whatever the score, so the child is never penalized by the game.
6. See the summary: results for each word, cued responses counted separately, and a button to copy the summary into your own notes.

The center never listens to the child or scores speech automatically. Only the grown-up scores. It keeps no child accounts and saves nothing about the child: the summary disappears when the page is refreshed or closed.

### Preview mode and approval

Two things are kept separate:

- **Accepted for development:** the pair may be used to build and try the games. All 103 pairs are in this state.
- **Clinically approved:** Jana has approved the pair. None is yet.

Pairs awaiting approval appear only in **preview mode**, the private test version of the site. Preview mode shows a yellow "Preview mode" banner, an "Awaiting Jana's approval" label on each pair, and a "Preview" note on the game screen and in the summary. The public website shows only approved pairs. Until Jana approves some, every pattern on the public site says "Coming soon."

## 4. Words to check for pronunciation or suitability

### Pronunciation depends on the speaker
- **bag, beg** (in *bag / bad*, *beg / bed*): some regions say these with an "ay" vowel, which breaks the pair.
- **catch** (*catch / cat*): also said "ketch", which breaks the pair.
- **wheat** (*sweet / wheat*): some speakers start it with an /h/, which breaks the pair.
- **coop** (*scoop / coop*): also said with the vowel of "book".
- **thought / taught**, **small / mall**: transcribed with the "aw" vowel. Speakers who say *cot* and *caught* the same use the "ah" vowel in both words, so each pair still holds for them.
- **four / pour**: for some speakers *pour* sounds like *poor*.
- **put / push**: transcribed with the vowel of "book".
- **but** (*buck / but*) and **can** (*scan / can*): usually unstressed in speech. They are transcribed in their full forms, and *can* means the tin can.

### Same sound, different meaning (decides the picture)
tea/tee, sea/see, two/to/too, dye/die, deer/dear (the animal is chosen), dough/doe, right/write, night/knight, no/know, led/lead, tick (insect or check mark).

### Hard to picture (abstract words)
came, got, but, late, lot, dole, taught, thought, fate, rate, might, nice, tell, fit, put, tone, mock, mart, kin, low, led, lug, peat, bid, tame.

### Possibly not right for young children
- **dumb** (*gum / dumb*): can be heard as an insult.
- **fat** (*fat / pat*): kept. The picture must be a chubby cat or another nonhuman subject, never a person.
- **slim** (*slim / limb*): body-related.
- **dye** (*guy / dye*): sounds the same as "die".
- **slap** (*slap / lap*): hitting.
- **dole** (*goal / dole*): unfamiliar to children.
- **kate** (*skate / kate*): a person's name. The site can only show words in lowercase, so it appears as "kate".
- Smaller concerns: scar, smash, bite.

## 7. The games

| Game | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Listening Game** (optional, listed first) | The child taps the speaker and hears one of the two words, then taps the matching picture. The pictures unlock only after the word has played. Taps are scored automatically and reported separately from speech results. It is never required before the other games. It is switched off until both words in the pair have a picture and a recording Jana has checked, so **it cannot be tried yet**. |
| **Say the Word** | Both pictures side by side, with an arrow pointing to the word to say. After **Start** it runs by itself, so a child can play alone: a new word every 3 seconds by default (2, 4, or 5 to choose), and the arrow disappears for the last second so a repeated word points again. Each turn lights a star. Grown-up scoring is optional; with it on, the arrow waits for a score. |
| **Bubble Pop** | A bright coral reef, with small pictures of both words in the top corner. After **Start**, a bubble rises from a random spot on the sea floor every 3 seconds by default (2, 4, or 5 to choose) and takes half a second longer than that to reach the top, so several can be on screen at once. The child says each word and pops its bubble, trying to beat it to the top; a bubble that gets there hovers until popped. Grown-up scoring is optional; with it on, the bubble whose turn it is glows and a score pops it. |
| **Feed the Monster** | Munch, a friendly purple monster, waits with an open mouth. The child says the word, then taps the snack to feed Munch, and Munch's tummy fills up with each turn. In 20 turns, each word comes up 10 times. |
| **Spin & Say** | A wheel shows both words. The child spins it, and it always lands on the word for that turn; the word and picture appear only then. |
| **Treasure Reveal** | Tiles cover a treasure island, and each turn lifts one. When the last tile goes, the treasure chest opens. |
| **Sound Train** | Each turn adds a car carrying that word's picture and moves the train one stop closer to the station. |

In every game, **Incorrect** and **Cued** move the game forward just like **Correct**, and **Undo** puts the game back one step.

## 8. How Jana can test each game

The site manager starts preview mode on a computer with the project (`npm run dev`) and opens **http://localhost:5173/#/phonological**. Alternatively, they can make a private review copy with `VITE_MINIMAL_PAIRS_PREVIEW=true` set when building it. A yellow "Preview mode" banner confirms that preview is on.

For each game:

1. On the first screen, choose a pattern. Then tap **Beginning of the word** or **End of the word** on a sound's card, and choose a word pair. *key / tea* is a good first pair, because both words have pictures.
2. Choose the game, then tick **Fewer turns: 5 of each word**.
3. Play a few turns, tapping **Correct**, **Incorrect**, and **Cued** at least once each. Check that the game moves forward every time.
4. Tap **Undo** and check that the game goes back one step: one fewer star, bubble, tile, or train car.
5. On a computer, try the keys: **1** Correct, **2** Incorrect, **3** Cued, **Backspace** Undo.
6. Finish all turns, tap **See how it went**, and check the counts for each word. Cued is shown on its own.
7. Tap **Copy summary for my notes** and paste it somewhere to check the text.
8. Try one session of the default **20 turns**, and try the same pair under **Backing** to confirm the other word becomes the target.
9. Try a phone or tablet. Everything should fit, with the scoring buttons along the bottom.

Things to look at along the way:

- **Spin & Say:** the word stays hidden until the wheel stops.
- **Feed the Monster** and **Bubble Pop:** tapping the snack or bubble is optional fun and never changes the score.
- **Listening Game:** shows "Not ready yet" and lists what it needs. It cannot be played until recordings are made and checked.
- **Review status:** the **Review status** button in the yellow banner opens a page listing every pair with its approval, transcription, picture, and recording status. Each row has a **Try it** button.
